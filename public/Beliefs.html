What We Teach

Our Beliefs

The truths that shape everything we do as a ministry.

I

The Word of God

We believe the Bible is the inspired, authoritative Word of God, given as the foundation for faith and daily living.

II

One God, Three Persons

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

III

Salvation Through Christ

We believe salvation is a gift of God's grace, received through faith in the death and resurrection of Jesus Christ.

IV

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the present-day work of the Holy Spirit, empowering believers to live, serve, and walk in God's purpose.

V

The Church

We believe the Church is the body of Christ, called together in fellowship, worship, and service to one another and the world.

VI

Prayer & Healing

We believe in the power of prayer and God's continued work of healing and restoration in the lives of His people.
