Since Our Founding

Our Story

The journey of faith, family, and community that brought us here.

Founder · Est. 2010

Apostle Rose Mung'afu

In 2010, Apostle Rose Mung'afu answered a calling to build a home for the broken-hearted, the searching, and the faithful alike. With nothing but a handful of believers, an unwavering trust in God, and a heart devoted to prayer, she planted the seed that would grow into Shalom Lord Is Peace Ministry.

Guided by her deep conviction in the power of God's Word and her tireless commitment to discipleship, Apostle Rose has shepherded this ministry through every season with grace, wisdom, and an enduring love for God's people. Her legacy is one of faith that moves mountains and a family built on peace — true to the very meaning of Shalom.

Growing Together

A Family That Grew

As word spread and more families joined us, the ministry grew beyond what we imagined — new ministries were formed for children, youth, and families, and our services expanded to meet the needs of a growing congregation. Through every season, our commitment to God's word and to one another never wavered.

Today

Where We Are Now

Today, Shalom Lord Is Peace Ministry stands as a home for all — a place where people come as they are and leave transformed. With services throughout the week, active ministries for every age, and a heart for our community, we continue to grow in faith and love.

Looking Ahead

Our Vision

To be a light in our community and beyond — raising a generation rooted in faith, walking in peace, and living out God's purpose for their lives, one family at a time.
