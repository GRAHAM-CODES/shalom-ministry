Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know before you visit or get involved.

What time are your services?
We gather on Wednesdays and Saturdays from 3:00–5:00 PM, and Sundays across three services: Morning Glory (7:00–8:00 AM), English Service (8:30–10:00 AM), and Family Service (10:30 AM–1:00 PM). See the full Service Times page for details.
What should I wear?
Come as you are. There's no dress code here — whether you're dressed up or came straight from home, you'll fit right in.
Do you have a ministry for kids?
Yes! Our Kids Ministry offers a safe, fun, age-appropriate experience for children during service. Learn more on our Ministries page.
How can I get involved or join a small group?
We'd love that. Visit our Ministries page to learn about our Groups ministry, or reach out through our Prayer Request page and our team will follow up with you.
How do I give or tithe?
You can give via Lipa Na M-Pesa — full details, including our Paybill and Account number, are on our Give page.
Can I watch services online?
Yes, you can watch our latest messages anytime from the homepage, or tune in live during service hours.
How do I submit a prayer request?
Visit our Prayer Request page and fill out the short form. Every request is kept confidential and read only by our pastoral team.
Where are you located?
Visit our Plan Your Visit page for our address and everything you need to know before you come.
